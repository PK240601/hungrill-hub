Our Story

Crafting Sizzling Perfection on the Grill

Welcome to Hungrill Hub, Irinjalakuda's favorite spot for flame-kissed Arabian and American fast food. Located at the heart of C. V. Arcade on Railway Station Road, we are dedicated to serving meals cooked with passion, smoke, and custom-blended spices.

From our crowd-favorite Kansas-style Al Faham to our legendary thick bread Iranian Shawarma rolls, every dish is prepared fresh using handpicked ingredients to ensure you get that authentic, juicy, fire-grilled flavor in every bite.
